Claudio's eyes

The buddy sits inside the printed Claudio case. Its screen turns orange and becomes Claudio's face: pixel eyes that react to what the agents are doing, step aside for permission and question screens, and share the sleep rotation with the clock and Mac cards.

What each state does

Idle
Blinks every 2–6 s, sometimes twice; looks left, right, up, or sweeps the room; one eye narrows when curious.
Working
Lids lowered and tilted in (concentrating); reads lines left to right, glances up to think, twitches while "typing". Blinks less.
Waiting
Inner lids raised (worried), eyes a cell lower; glances at you and to the sides, nervous double blinks, small sighs.
Task done
^ ^ eyes with a bounce and sparkles, sometimes a wink; back to idle after 4 s.
Asleep
Closed ‿ ‿ eyes breathing one cell up and down, a pixel "z", now and then one eye peeks.
Interrupted
Eyes widen and look down at the request, then the permission or question card slides up; the eyes ride along in the card's corner tile, worried, and come back with a reaction: happy after an approval or answer, a blink after Deny, a glance after Later.

Each beat is chosen at random from the state's pool, so two minutes of "working" never loop the same way. Gaze moves in whole 8 px cells, like the case's pixel grid.

To decide before the firmware

  1. Where the face lives. (a) A new first view "Face" before Home; the buddy goes back to it after 20 s without a touch, a tap opens Home. (b) The face replaces Home and Home's text moves to the Agents view. (c) The face only on the sleep screen. Recommended: (a), nothing on Home is lost.
  2. Sleep background. Orange keeps Claudio looking awake at 20 % brightness, but a full orange screen draws far more power than black on an AMOLED and wears the panel evenly lit for hours. Ember (dark background, orange eyes and text) reads as "Claudio dozing". Recommended: ember.
  3. The alert's corner. The approved permission screen shows the pet in its corner; here it shows Claudio's eyes in an orange tile. Recommended: eyes, the pet stays on Agents and Home.
  4. Eye spacing. Clawd's eyes sit wider than the panel; the default scales them in to x 100 / 380 px. The spread slider shows closer-set options.
  5. Per-session pets. With the face on top, the main pet appears less; session pets (task #14) stay as they are on Agents and in the sheets.

Nothing here changes the contract: the face reads the same pet state the firmware already derives (idle, working, waiting, happy, sleep).

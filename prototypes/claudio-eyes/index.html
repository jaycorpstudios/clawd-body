Claudio's eyes

The buddy sits inside the printed Claudio case. Its screen turns orange and becomes Claudio's face: pixel eyes that react to what the agents are doing, step aside for permission and question screens, and share the sleep rotation with the clock and Mac cards.

What each state does

Idle
Blinks every 2–6 s, sometimes twice; looks left, right, up, or sweeps the room; one eye narrows when curious; now and then calm ▪ ▪ dots.
Working
Seven looks, never the same one twice in a row: heads down reading line by line (flat half lids), deep focus □ □, pushing through > <, thinking it over (glance up, one eye a little narrower), typing (small bob), tracking the output ▪ ▪, a slow blink. Lids stay flat: no slant, so it reads focused, not cross.
Waiting
Outer half of each eye one stroke lower (worried), eyes a cell lower; glances at you and to the sides, nervous double blinks, small sighs.
Task done
^ ^ eyes with a bounce and sparkles, sometimes a wink (▮ –) or heart eyes; back to idle after 4 s.
Asleep
Closed ‿ ‿ eyes breathing one cell up and down, a pixel "z", now and then one eye peeks.
Interrupted
Eyes widen and look down at the request, then the permission or question card slides up; the eyes ride along in the card's corner tile, worried, and come back with a reaction: happy after an approval or answer, a blink after Deny, a glance after Later.

Each beat is chosen at random from the state's pool, never repeating the last one. Gaze moves in whole 8 px cells, every line is the same two cells thick (1.3 mm) and diagonals step one cell at a time, so strokes never thin out. Shapes follow the printed Claudio expressions: ▪ ▪, > <, □ □, hearts, wink, ^ ^.
